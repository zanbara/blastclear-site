DEFINITION

What BlastClear does

BlastClear builds the clearance perimeter of a blast from the ballistic dome matching the maximum radius you set. The reach is then graded sector by sector around the blast: full to the front, where the throw goes, reduced laterally, reduced further to the rear.

The dome is intersected with the actual site topography. The perimeter therefore follows the ground, its benches and its breaks of slope, rather than a theoretical horizontal plane.

All three families of blast are handled in the same project: production on a closed contour, pre-splitting on polylines, secondary and boulder blasting on points. Several blasts of the same round merge into a single perimeter, with no manual rework. The result comes out as a PDF drawing ready to sign, as DXF for the site drawings, or as an image.

BEFORE

Topography in one tool, contours in another. The perimeter stays a circle: no 3D dome ; and when there is one, the topography intersections and the merging of perimeters are still handled by hand, then the plan is redrawn. Four tools, half a day, and a clearance zone that remains an estimate built on centroids and offsets.

DOME OR CIRCLE

Why not simply a circle

A circle applies the same distance in every direction, over ground that never is flat. On this deep blast, at the same 500 m radius, it covers 78 hectares where the dome intersected with the topography holds 36. It ties up twice the ground, and still falls short of the dome where the terrain drops away.

DOME COMPUTATION

  • Domes matched to the real blast contour (DXF), not generic circles
  • Reduction sectors: e.g. rear −30 %, lateral −15 %, front not reduced
  • Automatic intersection with drone topography (GeoTIFF texture supported)
  • Blast orientation detected from the initiation timing
  • Built-in radius simulator

  • Multi-blast merge with no manual rework

  • Forward-looking simulations for pits under study
